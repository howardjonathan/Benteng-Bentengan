# Benteng-Bentengan
C++ Card Game
_____________________________________________________________________

Basic Rules:

-Each player has 5 life cards and 5 hand cards

-Life cards can only have number cards, no face cards

-Hand cards are used to attack your opponent's life cards or defend your own life cards

-All life cards start unrevealed

-You will be able to see your own life cards

-Attacking an opponent's life card with a numbered card reveals the life card and adds the life card with the card you just placed

-Life cards that have been attacked stay revealed

-Life cards that eventually reach a multiple of 10 after the value being added will die

-Killing a life card will remove the life card and all the added cards onto that life card

-Using 10 as your life card is allowed, attacking with 10 is allowed too

-In every turn you are only allowed to play 1 card

-'Removed' cards will return into the main deck and will be shuffled

-After every turn you will take a card from the main deck of cards

-The cards are using the classic 52 cards, no duplicates

-Face cards have unique abilities

_____________________________________________________________________

Face Card Abilities:

Jack:

-Use this to defend your own life cards

-When attacking an unrevealed life card with a Jack, it will not reveal the life card and instead only remove the Jack

-Attacking a revealed life card with a Jack will only remove the Jack without affecting the life card


Queen:

-Swaps life cards with opponents

-You may pick which one of your life cards to swap

-You may pick which one of your opponent's life cards to swap

-Swapping a life card that has a Jack will swap the Jack along with the life card


King:

-Instantly kills a life

-Using a King onto a Jack kills the life even with the Jack

Balance Change Ideas:

-Kills the last 3 cards placed onto a life card (including the life card).

-A Jack on the life card will reduce that count to the last 2 cards.
